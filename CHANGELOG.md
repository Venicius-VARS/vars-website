# VARS Website — Change Log

## 2026-10-29 — Institutional v2

### Strategy and content
- Strengthened positioning around aerospace engineering services, FPGA, embedded software, EGSE, integration, verification and testing.
- Clarified engineering service descriptions in English and Brazilian Portuguese.
- Added a three-stage engineering approach: understand, engineer/integrate, verify/document.
- Linked service cards to the contact section.
- Avoided customer names, mission details, certifications and unapproved case studies.

### Design and accessibility
- Refined dark corporate aerospace visual style and subtle technical grid.
- Improved responsive card layout and engineering workflow section.
- Retained keyboard focus indicators, skip link, language buttons and reduced-motion support.
- Preserved existing VARS branding and images.

### Discoverability
- Updated page title and description.
- Added canonical URL, Open Graph metadata, robots.txt and sitemap.xml.

### Publishing
- Updated the main branch, as authorized by the owner.
- Kept CNAME, .nojekyll and email DNS untouched.
- No new framework, tracking, analytics or external dependency introduced.

### Validation and limitations
- Repository content inspection confirmed all HTML data-i keys have corresponding JavaScript translation keys.
- Visual browser, screen-reader, Lighthouse, cross-browser, and production HTTP checks have not been completed.
- The EN/PT language switch is client-side; search engines primarily index the English default. Dedicated localized URLs and hreflang are future improvements.
- Competitor references inform positioning only; no competitor designs or text were copied.
